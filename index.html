🏝️ Real World Traction

Field-Tested in Lamu County, Kenya

Lamu Island is known for its rich Swahili heritage, but schools face persistent challenges: intermittent electricity, high cellular data costs, and scarce physical textbooks.

Working directly with a partner teacher on the island, we deployed Ubuntu Elimu across two school computer labs equipped with 20+ laptops each. Students come in during free periods, open their digitized CBC textbooks, and learn with an AI tutor that never asks for an internet connection.

"Before Ubuntu Elimu, if the cellular mast in the area went down during rainy weather, our computer lab sat idle. Now, forty students sit down at once and study biology, physics, and history without worrying about a single bundle expiring."

— Partner Secondary Teacher & CBC Curriculum Champion, Lamu County

Simple & Transparent

How Does It Work Without Internet?

We solved the hardware barrier using our open-source Denizen AI Engine.

1

Upload School Syllabus

Teachers load approved PDF textbooks, class revision notes, or past exams once onto the phone or computer.

2

On-Device Indexing

The app turns pages into compact vector representations stored inside an embedded local database.

3

Student Asks Question

The student asks any study question in English or Swahili. The app instantly pulls the exact matching textbook paragraph.

4

Socratic Explanation

Our compact 0.5B model breaks down the concept step-by-step, generating flashcards and revision quizzes with zero hallucination.
